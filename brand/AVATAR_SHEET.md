## Key challenges & pain points

**1. Severe cervical muscular ischemia & "coat hanger" pain**
- Sustained forward-flexed screen posture creates up to 60 lbs of gravitational load on the neck, forcing "Cinderella" muscle fibers into non-stop contraction.
- Microvascular capillary strangulation triggers cellular hypoxia, ATP depletion, and locked actin-myosin crossbridges — rock-hard trigger points.
- Searing, burning ache radiates across the upper trapezius and cervicothoracic junction into suboccipital tension headaches behind the eyes by ~4:15 PM daily.

**2. Superficial symptom-masking & remedy fatigue**
- Heating pads/menthol creams penetrate only 1–3 mm, failing deep ischemic knots while risking "toasted skin syndrome" (Erythema Ab Igne).
- Percussion massage guns and rigid traction wedges trigger muscular guarding reflexes, cervical nerve irritation, and inner-ear vertigo (BPPV).
- In-office PT/chiropractic/massage require high recurring co-pays (~$200/session) with relief fading within 24–48 hours of returning to desk work.

**3. Relational exhaustion & domestic friction**
- Chronic pain depletes cognitive reserves by 5:00 PM — low patience, snapping at children/partner over minor friction.
- End-of-day sensory overstimulation causes touch aversion, pulling away from physical affection.
- High domestic workload prevents dedicating hours to clinic visits — guilt and exhaustion.

## Goals & aspirations

**Short-term:** Dissolve trapezius knots, end the daily burning ache/tension headaches; adopt a quick, wireless, hands-free 15-minute at-home routine; transition from work to family time with calm energy.

**Long-term:** Break free from dependency on pain pills/heating pads/recurring appointments; protect long-term career stamina; reclaim physical comfort and mobility for vacations and family life without feeling prematurely aged.
